RutRoh builds hosted operations cockpits for 1–25 person service businesses — quoting, scheduling, invoicing, payment follow-up, and customer messaging in one place, customized to how each business actually runs.

What your cockpit does

Quoting & follow-up

Estimates go out fast, and polite follow-ups go out on schedule — so quotes stop dying in a text thread.

Scheduling & reminders

Jobs on one calendar, with confirmations and appointment reminders your customers actually read.

Invoicing & payments

Invoices on job completion and courteous payment follow-up, so owners stop chasing checks at 9pm.

Customer messaging

Every customer-facing message is drafted for the owner and sent only with their approval — never on autopilot.

Review requests

Happy customers get a well-timed ask for a review; unhappy ones get the owner's attention first.

Owner's daily digest

One morning summary of the day ahead: schedule, money in, money owed, and what needs a decision.

How it works

Tell us how you run

A short interview about your jobs, pricing, crew, and customers — in person or online, at your pace.

We build your cockpit

Your own private app, on your own web address, configured to your services, your prices, and your way of working.

You approve, it runs

The cockpit drafts the busywork; you approve what goes out. Quiet hours and opt-outs are respected by design.

Messaging & consent

Text messages are sent only to people who opted in — customers who gave their phone number to a business we serve and consented to receive messages about that service.

  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help.
  • Message frequency varies by your interaction with the business. Message and data rates may apply.
  • No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
