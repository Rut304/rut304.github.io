Back-office software for small service businesses.

What we do

RutRoh builds hosted "cockpit" apps for 1–25 person service businesses — landscapers, cleaning crews, housekeeping teams, and other local trades. One place for the work that eats an owner's evenings: photo-based quoting, quote follow-ups, scheduling and appointment reminders, invoicing and payment follow-up, review requests, and a daily owner digest.

How it works

  • Each customer gets their own cockpit, customized to how their business actually runs.
  • Every customer-facing message is drafted for the owner and sent only with their approval.
  • Messaging respects quiet hours and opt-outs by design — reply STOP to any text to opt out.
